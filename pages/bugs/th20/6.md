# Uninitialized ANM
[hr_major]

## [specs]  
[hr_minor]

* **Versions**: [yes]1.00a[/yes]
* **Difficulty**: [yes]Easy[/yes] - [yes]Normal[/yes] - [yes]Hard[/yes] - [yes]Lunatic[/yes] - [yes]Extra[/yes]
* **Mode**: [yes]Main game[/yes] - [yes]Practice mode[/yes] - [yes]Spell practice[/yes]  
* **Shottype**: [no]Reimu[/no] - [yes]Marisa[/yes]

## [what]
[hr_minor]

Bullets have a chance to fail to spawn, bullets can be rotated weirdly or have the wrong sprite, or Marisa's laser can bug out, in which her right-most laser has the possibility to deactivate.

[img=bugs/th20/6_laser.png, figtitle=A screenshot of Marisa's right-most option being weird, alt=A screenshot of Marisa firing lasers.]

## [how]
[hr_minor]

This behaviour happens at random and is out of control for any player. Marisa's laser(s) rarely bug out when it comes in contact with an enemy. This event happens at random. 

## [why_idk]
[hr_minor]

This is caused due to uninitialized ANM. For example, bullets using a common bullet transformation have a chance to not spawn because the bullet transformation includes an uninitialized variable. Depending on the value there is a chance bullets may not spawn or for bullets to have a weird sprite.
